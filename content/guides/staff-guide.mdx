<h2 id="hire-and-manage">How to hire and review staff</h2>

The focused UI material accepted in the third course shows two separate tablet areas: **Hire** for recruiting staff and **Manage** for reviewing employees already working in the park. This is a dated UI observation rather than an official manual, so labels may move after an interface update or differ on consoles.

Use the Hire section when you still have capacity. If the game says the cap has been reached, the next question is capacity research or Staff Overflow—not a hidden second hiring menu.

<h2 id="capacity">How staff capacity and Staff Overflow fit together</h2>

Two independent UI videos place hiring-level upgrades at the **Research Table**. A separate Staff Overflow route allows hiring above the normal cap with an additional cost.

This page intentionally does not publish a level-by-level cost table or an overflow percentage. The checked material did not provide a current official table, and older economy values can become wrong after balance patches. The durable answer is the route: research for normal capacity, or use Staff Overflow only after reading the current in-game cost.

<h2 id="security">Security is now a staff role</h2>

Older guides may say staff cannot deal with bad visitors. That answer became outdated with version 1.0, which added **Security** as a staff type that removes bad visitors. The same release added **Mascots**, described as increasing visitor happiness.

Update 1.0.43 added the **Saboteur** trait. The official patch note describes Saboteurs as arriving with a sledgehammer and unplugging a pool if they are not stopped. That is a verified version change, but it is not enough to invent a complete visual-identification guide, Security success rate, or ideal guard count.

Update 1.0.50 reduced the percentage chance of a Saboteur appearing. The official note does not state the new percentage, so this guide keeps that number unknown instead of estimating it.

<h2 id="staff-quitting">Why staff quit after version 1.0.43</h2>

Update 1.0.43 introduced quitting after a player bullies a staff member heavily within a short period. A quitting employee sends a notification, receives a red exclamation mark by their name, and is highlighted red.

Update 1.0.48 narrowed that behavior: staff do not quit on **Easy** or **Sandbox**, and an accessibility option can disable staff quitting and NPC retaliation. These details come from the Steam/PC patch stream; console parity remains unconfirmed in the sources checked for this page.

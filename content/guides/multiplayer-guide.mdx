<h2 id="player-limits">Official player limits by platform</h2>

The strongest current sources agree on a maximum session size of four. This corrects a six-player claim found in a third-party guide during the third-course audit; the official release announcement and console listings take priority.

| Platform | What the checked source confirms | Evidence date and limit |

These rows describe the correct CayPlay game entity and the wording visible in the named sources. They do not prove that all three platforms can connect to one another.

<h2 id="hosting-and-joining">What is verified about hosting and joining</h2>

The official 1.0 announcement says a host can run a park from the beginning or load an existing park, then play with up to three friends or strangers. Optional proximity voice chat was included with the multiplayer launch.

The checked official material confirms that joining exists, but it does not provide a current button-by-button guide for every platform. This page therefore does not invent menu names, password-room steps, firewall fixes, or administrator-mode advice. If the current menu differs from older videos or third-party guides, follow the in-game multiplayer prompts.

<h2 id="host-controls">Host controls added after launch</h2>

- **Update 1.0.30:** improved the host permission system for building, destroying, hiring staff, and opening the park. Joining players start with a hammer rather than a sledgehammer, and first-time multiplayer users receive an explanation of the ticket-booth settings.
- **Update 1.0.43:** added lobby-only player bans and more host settings, including painting and Caymart purchasing options.
- **Update 1.0.48:** added safeguards against bad actors spoofing Steam IDs and let all players move pools that contain blobs.

Those notes come from the Steam/PC update stream. They should not be read as proof that the same numbered build reached both console platforms at the same time.

<h2 id="crossplay">Is Waterpark Simulator crossplay?</h2>

**Crossplay is not verified by the official sources used for this page.** The official site, Steam page, PlayStation listing, and saved Xbox listing do not provide a crossplay statement. Until CayPlay or a platform page publishes a clear answer, plan multiplayer sessions within the same platform instead of assuming a mixed PC-and-console lobby will work.
